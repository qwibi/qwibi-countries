# Privacy

This App runs no server and collects nothing.

It publishes one thing: the outlines of the world's countries (Natural Earth, public domain). When
you add the App to My map and mark a country as visited, the mark is kept by Qwibi, belongs to you
alone, is shown only to you, and is erased with your account. Until you confirm an email, your
marks stay on your device; when you confirm one, Qwibi imports them into your account. The App's
developer cannot see your marks, your maps or who uses the App.

Qwibi's own terms and privacy policy apply to your account and your marks.
